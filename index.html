THE DAILY CEREMONY

THE 4-STEP LUXURY RITUAL

Consistency is the soul of dermaceutical efficacy. A disciplined two-minute ritual engineered to transform hair care into a restorative private meditation.

01

PREPARE

Begin with a clean, towel-dry or dry scalp. Part hair cleanly with fingers or a wide-tooth comb to expose the scalp skin along your targeted zone.

02

APPLY

Draw the elixir using the precision dropper. Dispense droplets directly onto the roots and scalp surface rather than hair strands to ensure direct dermal contact.

03

MASSAGE

Gently distribute using firm circular fingertip acupressure for 60 to 90 seconds. Stimulate local microcirculation until fully absorbed. Do not rinse.

04

REPEAT

Adopt as a non-negotiable twice-daily ritual — once in the morning and once before evening rest. Consistency drives follicular conditioning over time.
